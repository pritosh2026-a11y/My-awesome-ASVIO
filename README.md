# My-awesome-ASVIO
ASVIO - AI Search Visibility Intelligence & Optimization is a platform that i want to build not only for brands and websites to be ranked  but also to measure how they are represented, cited and included across the internet by various AI-generated search and LLM-powered experiences.

ASVIO is a vision for analytics in the coming AI era. Traditional web analytics and SEO focus on rankings, clicks, and traffic. As users increasingly discover information through LLM-powered search and AI-generated answers, a new layer of visibility is emerging: how a website or brand is represented, cited, contextualized, and included in those answers.

ASVIO aims to build the intelligence layer for that ecosystem by measuring AI visibility, citation share, claim representation, contextual inclusion, competitive presence, and opportunities for improvement across publicly observable AI/search experiences.
